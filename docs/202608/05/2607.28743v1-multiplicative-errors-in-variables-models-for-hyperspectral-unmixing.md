## 摘要
从高光谱图像中估计端元光谱及其相应的丰度是遥感中的一个基本逆问题。标准线性混合模型依赖于加性同方差误差，这错误地指定了光学测量固有的均值-方差关系，其中噪声强度可能与信号幅度成正比。这导致置信区间的覆盖率不佳。为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了一种分层贝叶斯乘法误差变量（EIV）模型。受光传播物理学的启发，我们的公式通过多元对数正态规范捕获信号相关噪声，该规范自然适应跨光谱波段的依赖性。EIV框架将端元特征视为随机变量，并允许类别特定的方差缩放，从而产生灵活且物理上合理的数据生成过程。我们确定了在乘法模型下丰度向量的MLE具有恒定的渐近多元变异系数，这意味着良好校准的置信区间应与信号幅度成比例缩放。相比之下，加性模型产生的区间宽度恒定，与信号幅度无关，导致当真实噪声为乘法时，在低丰度时过度覆盖，在高丰度时覆盖不足。完整分层模型的实际实现和推断通过模型参数的后验分布获得。在多个真实和模拟数据集上的实验表明，与标准加性模型相比，所提出的模型导出的置信区间在丰度水平上实现了更好的覆盖率，其宽度与信号幅度成比例缩放，同时实现相当或更优的信号重建误差。

## Abstract
Estimating endmember spectra and their corresponding abundances from hyperspectral images is a fundamental inverse problem in remote sensing. The standard linear mixing model relies on additive homoscedastic errors, which misspecifies the mean-variance relationship inherent to optical measurements for which the noise intensity might be proportional to the signal magnitude. This leads to confidence intervals with poor coverage. To address these limitations, we propose a hierarchical Bayesian multiplicative Errors-in-Variables (EIV) model. Motivated by the physics of light propagation, our formulation captures signal-dependent noise through a multivariate log-normal specification that naturally accommodates dependence across spectral bands. The EIV framework treats endmember signatures as random and allows for class-specific variance scaling, yielding a flexible and physically grounded data-generating process. We establish that the MLE of the abundance vector under the multiplicative model has a constant asymptotic multivariate coefficient of variation, implying that well-calibrated confidence intervals should scale proportionally with signal magnitude. The additive model, by contrast, produces intervals of constant width regardless of signal magnitude, leading to overcoverage at low abundance and undercoverage at high abundance when the true noise is multiplicative. Practical implementation and inference for the full hierarchical model is obtained from posterior distributions over the model parameters. Experiments on several real and simulated datasets demonstrate that confidence intervals derived from the proposed model achieve improved coverage across abundance levels compared to those from a standard additive model, with widths that scale proportionally with signal magnitude, while achieving comparable or superior signal reconstruction error.
